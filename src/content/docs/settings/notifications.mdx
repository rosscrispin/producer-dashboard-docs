Notifications keep you informed about what matters most in your production workflow. Whether you need to know the moment a collaborator leaves feedback or you want a reminder three days before a track is due, the Notifications settings let you stay in control.

This page lives within **Settings**, where you can also manage your profile, connected accounts, storage preferences, and billing. Each category is separate, so you can update your notification preferences without touching anything else.

---

## Accessing Notification Settings

To reach your notification preferences:

1. Open **Settings** from the main navigation or user menu
2. Select **Notifications** from the left sidebar
3. The notification panel opens showing all available options

On smaller screens, the sidebar becomes a collapsible menu. Tap the menu button to reveal the list of sections, then select Notifications.

---

## Notification Categories

Your notification preferences are organized into categories that match different aspects of your workflow. Each category can be toggled on or off, and some include additional options for finer control.

### Track Activity

These notifications inform you about changes to your tracks. You can receive alerts when:

- A collaborator adds or edits a comment
- A track's stage changes (for example, moving from "Editing" to "Ready to Post")
- Artwork or metadata gets updated
- A track is marked as published

As a producer, track activity notifications help you keep tabs on multiple songs without constantly checking each one manually. If you work with multiple artists or handle several projects at once, these alerts ensure nothing slips through the cracks.

### Collaborator Updates

Stay informed about the people working with you:

- New collaborator requests or invitations
- Collaborator acceptance or removal from a track
- Profile changes from your collaborators

These notifications are particularly useful when you're managing a team of vocalists, mix engineers, or feature artists. You won't miss when someone new joins a project or when responsibilities shift.

### Due Dates and Reminders

Never miss a deadline again:

- Reminders before a track's due date (choose 1, 3, or 7 days in advance)
- Alerts when a track's due date passes
- Overdue notifications for tracks that need immediate attention

Setting reminders for your tracks keeps your release schedule on track. Many producers find it helpful to receive a three-day warning before a deadline, giving enough time to handle any last-minute revisions.

### Project and Bucket Updates

When projects or playlists change:

- New tracks added to your projects
- Buckets renamed or reorganized
- Project-level announcements

### System Notifications

Keep your account secure and up to date:

- Security alerts (new sign-ins, password changes)
- Account verification messages
- Service announcements and maintenance notices

System notifications are typically enabled by default since they protect your account. You can adjust these, but it's generally recommended to keep security alerts active.

## How to Update Your Preferences

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Notifications**
2. Find the category you want to adjust
3. Toggle the main switch to enable or disable all notifications in that category
4. For categories with additional options, click the **gear icon** or expand the section to see granular controls
5. Click **Save Preferences** when finished

Your changes take effect immediately. If you're signed in on multiple devices, the preferences sync across all of them.

---

## In-App Notifications

Notifications appear within Producer Dashboard when you're signed in. A badge in the app header shows your unread count, and clicking it opens the notification panel with a chronological list of recent alerts.

---

## Tips

:::tip
For client-facing projects, consider enabling stage change notifications so your artists receive updates automatically. This reduces the back-and-forth messages asking "how's my track coming along?"
:::

:::tip
If you're working on a tight release schedule, enable the 1-day due date reminder for all tracks. It gives you just enough lead time to catch any issues before the deadline hits.
:::

## Troubleshooting

**Not receiving notifications?**

- Check that the specific category is toggled on in your preferences
- Verify your email address is correct in Profile settings
- Check your spam or junk folder for email notifications
- Ensure your browser or desktop app has notification permissions enabled
- Try signing out and back in to refresh the notification connection

**Receiving too many notifications?**

- Disable categories that don't apply to your workflow
