Social media links let you showcase your presence across the web on your public artist page. When fans visit your page, they can click straight through to your Instagram, SoundCloud, YouTube, or any other platform where you share your music.

This makes it easy for listeners to follow you wherever they already spend time online — no need to hunt for your profiles across different apps.

---

## What Platforms Can You Connect?

Producer Dashboard supports links to all the major platforms music creators use:

- **Instagram** — your artist or personal account
- **Twitter/X** — for updates and engagement
- **Facebook** — page or personal profile
- **YouTube** — your channel or specific videos
- **SoundCloud** — your mixes, singles, or full discography
- **Spotify** — artist profile link
- **Apple Music** — artist page
- **Bandcamp** — your music store page
- **TikTok** — your creator account
- **Website** — your own domain or landing page

You can add up to ten different links, giving you plenty of room to connect every platform where you have a presence.

---

## Adding Your Social Links

The social links section lives in your Settings area. Here's how to add your profiles:

1. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner of the app
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu
3. Find the **Social Media Links** section in the sidebar
4. Click on it to open the social links panel

### Adding a New Link

Once you're in the social links panel, adding a new profile is straightforward:

1. Click the **Add Link** button at the bottom of the links list
2. A new row appears with two fields: **Platform** and **URL**
3. Click the platform dropdown and select the service you're linking to
4. Paste your profile URL into the URL field
5. Click **Save** to confirm

The platform dropdown shows all available options alphabetically, so you can quickly find the service you need.

### Editing Existing Links

Want to change a URL or swap out a platform? Just click on any existing link row to expand it, then update the fields directly. Once you've made your changes, hit **Save** to lock them in.

If you want to rearrange your links, drag and drop the rows to reorder them. The order you set here determines how links appear on your public page — put your most important profiles first.

### Removing Links

To remove a link, click the trash icon on the right side of any link row. The link disappears immediately without needing to save. If you remove a link by accident, just add it again using the steps above.

---

## How Links Appear on Your Public Page

When fans visit your public artist page, your social links display as clickable icons in a row at the top or bottom of the page, depending on your page layout settings.

Each platform shows its recognizable icon — a camera for Instagram, a musical note for SoundCloud, and so on. Fans see the icon and can tap or click to visit that platform directly.

The links open in a new browser tab, so visitors don't lose their place on your artist page.

## Tips

:::tip
**Link to your artist profile specifically.** When adding Spotify or Apple Music links, make sure you're linking to your official artist page rather than a single album or playlist. This gives fans access to your complete discography.
:::

:::tip
**Keep URLs accurate.** Double-check that your links go to the right place. A broken link frustrates fans and makes you look inattentive. Test each link after saving to confirm it works.
:::

:::tip
**Prioritize your active platforms.** If you post to Instagram daily but only update Twitter occasionally, put Instagram first. This highlights where fans are most likely to engage with fresh content.
:::

:::tip
**Include your website last.** Your own website is valuable, but most fans will follow you on social platforms they already use. Save your website for the end of your links so it acts as a final destination for superfans who want more.
:::
