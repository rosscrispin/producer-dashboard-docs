The AI Assistant in Producer Dashboard understands natural language, so you can ask questions and give commands the way you'd naturally talk about your music. This page shows you concrete examples of queries that work well with the assistant.

Whether you're managing a handful of tracks or running a full catalog, these examples help you get the most out of the conversational interface.

---

## Track Stage and Property Commands

The assistant excels at updating multiple tracks at once using plain English. Here are queries that handle track properties:

**Change workflow stages**
- "Set the stage to Finished for all tracks in the Summer Album bucket"
- "Move the selected tracks to Idea stage"
- "Mark everything in Ideas as Ready to Post"

**Update excitement and ratings**
- "Set excitement to 85% for tracks tagged with Remix"
- "Set the energy level to 70% for the selected song"
- "Rate the high-priority tracks at 90% excitement"

**Rename and organize**
- "Remove versioning from the track names"
- "Rename this track to Sunset Dreams"
- "Add the word Radio to all tracks in the Pop bucket"

**Favourites and archiving**
- "Mark the selected tracks as favourite"
- "Archive all tracks older than 6 months"
- "Unfavourite everything in the backlog bucket"

## Tag Management Queries

Tags help you categorize and filter your music. The assistant can create, apply, and remove tags across your entire catalog:

**Apply and remove tags**
- "Add the Remix tag to the selected tracks"
- "Apply the WIP tag to everything without a stage"
- "Remove all tags from tracks older than 3 months"
- "Tag the high-energy tracks as Festival Ready"

**Create and manage tag definitions**
- "Create a tag called Client Delivery"
- "Rename the Needs Review tag to Pending Feedback"
- "Delete the obsolete Old Tag"

**Tag-based queries**
- "Show me all tracks with the Hip-Hop tag"
- "Which tracks have both the Remix and VIP tags?"
- "Find tracks without any tags assigned"

---

## Bucket and Project Organization

Buckets hold your tracks like folders in a music library. You can create and reorganize buckets through conversation:

**Create and rename buckets**
- "Create a bucket called Summer Tour Setlist"
- "Rename Ideas to Experiments"
- "Make a new bucket for client deliverables"

**Organize tracks between buckets**
- "Move the selected tracks to the Album 2024 bucket"
- "Move all finished tracks to Published"
- "Copy these tracks to the Backup Archive bucket"

**Manage bucket properties**
- "Change the Ideas bucket color to blue"
- "Set the Album bucket category to LP"

---

## Collaborator and Split Management

When working with other musicians and producers, the assistant helps you manage credits and royalty splits:

**Add and remove collaborators**
- "Add Sarah as a vocalist with 30% split"
- "Make Alex a co-producer on the selected track"
- "Remove the old collaborator from the track"
- "Add drummer credits to the rock songs"

**Manage splits**
- "Set splits to 60/40 between me and Jordan"
- "Show me the current splits for the selected tracks"
- "Update splits to 50/25/25 for the three producers"
- "Get collaborator info for all tracks in the Album bucket"

## Todo and Task Tracking

Keep your production workflow organized by creating and managing todos directly through the assistant:

**Create todos**
- "Add a todo to finish the vocals by next Friday"
- "Create a mixing checklist for the selected tracks"
- "Add a reminder to send the stems to mastering"

**Complete and manage todos**
- "Mark the vocal mixing todo as complete"
- "Complete all todos for this track"
- "Delete the outdated todo about old revisions"
- "Show me todos for the selected song"

## Stats and Analytics Queries

Get insights into your production habits, track completion patterns, and catalog health:

**Productivity tracking**
- "How many tracks did I finish this month?"
- "What's my average time from Idea to Published?"
- "Show me my production stats for the past quarter"

**Track insights**
- "Find tracks similar to this one"
- "Which tracks are overdue?"
- "What percentage of my tracks are in the Finished stage?"

**Release planning**
- "Show me my release timeline"
- "Which tracks are ready for release?"
- "Suggest an EP grouping from my best tracks"

**File analysis**
- "How many bounce files do I have?"
- "Show me file stats by bucket"
- "Which tracks are missing project files?"

## Saved Views and Filtering

Saved views remember your favorite filter combinations. Ask the assistant to create, apply, or switch between them:

**Apply saved views**
- "Show me the Ready to Release view"
- "Apply the High Priority filters"
- "Switch to the Client Work view"

**Create and manage views**
- "Create a view for high excitement tracks"
- "Save this filter as My Weekly Review"
- "Update the default view to include all columns"
- "Delete the old archived view"

## Tips

### Be Specific About Scope

The assistant understands context from your current selection. Saying "set these tracks to finished" uses your highlighted tracks. Saying "all tracks in the Rock bucket" applies to the entire bucket.

### Use Natural Stage Names

The assistant recognizes your workflow stages as you named them. If you renamed "In Progress" to "Editing," use "set to Editing" rather than the system default names.

### Ask for Confirmation on Bulk Actions

When updating many tracks at once, the assistant asks you to confirm before making changes. This prevents accidental bulk updates to your entire catalog.

### Combine Filters with Actions

You can chain criteria together: "set excitement to 90% for all tracks in the Album bucket that don't have the Final tag."
