The audio player in Producer Dashboard is a single consistent interface that appears across different parts of the app. While it doesn't have named modes or a context switcher, its behavior adapts slightly based on where you're using it.

---

## Tracks Page

When you select a track in the main grid, the player loads in the activity panel. You can play, pause, seek, and navigate between tracks using the standard playback controls. The waveform, timecode display, and transport buttons are all available here.

---

## Share Pages

When recipients open one of your share links, they see the same player interface. The waveform loads, playback works the same way, and any timecoded comments you or collaborators have left appear on the waveform as markers.

Recipients can click waveform markers to jump directly to that timestamp — useful when you've left feedback on a specific section of the track.
